In the room — everyone's selections apply to all:
    Controls
    • Trigger on a value, a bar, a tower or a point: select (click again to deselect)
    • Stick while pointing at a filter pane: scroll · at the globe or the cloud: rotate
    • Grip on the globe or the cloud: grab it and spin it
    • Left stick: walk · right stick: turn 30°
    • B back · A forward · Y clear all · X return to the centre
    • AR on a table: point at a surface and pull the trigger (or tap) to place the room · left stick: turn and resize it · X: move it again
    • On PC: drag to look around, WASD to move (E/C up, down), click to select, mouse wheel to scroll, ⌫ back
    • On a phone: drag to look around, joystick to move, tap to select, drag a filter pane to scroll it, Gyroscope to look around by moving the phone
    • The buttons at the bottom take you to each visualisation; Follow shows what someone else is seeing
